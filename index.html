What's automated, what's you

A clear line between what this platform does on its own and what still needs a person — nothing runs unsupervised where judgment actually matters.

Handled automatically

  • Drafting the first request to a vendor from the need you enter
  • Parsing a vendor's reply into structured availability, rate, and terms
  • Following up after silence, on a bounded schedule, so nothing goes quiet forever
  • Counter-offering once a quote comes in above budget, up to a capped number of rounds
  • Confirming a booking the moment terms are clearly acceptable
  • Tracking hold expiry deadlines and flagging ones about to lapse
  • Fanning one need out to several vendors and ranking confirmed offers by price
  • Splitting a single reply that answers more than one line item at once
  • Registering vendors in the directory automatically and tracking their real confirm rate
  • Routing each outbound message to email or WhatsApp based on the vendor's channel

Still needs you

  • Any reply that's ambiguous, partial, or a counter-offer the agent can't resolve on its own
  • Deciding your actual walk-away price and negotiation rules — the defaults are placeholders
  • Marking a confirmed hold as truly locked in once you've actually paid or booked
  • Reopening a confirmed booking when a client detail changes after the fact
  • Sending the message yourself if SMTP isn't connected — the draft is ready either way
  • Choosing between two ranked offers in a sourcing comparison — price isn't the only factor
  • Recording a WhatsApp reply yourself — there's no inbound polling for WhatsApp the way IMAP works for email
  • Picking which confirmed requests go into a client proposal, and reviewing it before it's sent

System status

What's actually wired up versus running in mock mode.

Mock mode uses rule-based stand-ins for drafting and parsing so the platform runs without an API key. Set ANTHROPIC_API_KEY, SMTP_*, and IMAP_* environment variables to make dispatch and inbox polling real.
