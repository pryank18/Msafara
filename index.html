The problem

You're running a network by hand

The coordination work doesn't show up on an invoice, but it eats the week.

01

A vendor goes quiet mid-negotiation

The thread stalls, nobody follows up on schedule, and the deal drifts until it's too late to recover.

02

A hold deadline slips past

A locked rate expires because no one was watching the clock across a dozen open threads at once.

03

Comparing quotes means reopening ten tabs

The side-by-side comparison lives in your head and a scratch notepad, not on a screen.

04

Confirmed bookings get retyped by hand

Numbers move from an email into a spreadsheet into a proposal document, one typo at a time.

How it works

Three steps, no new workflow to learn

01

Set your requirements and your rules

Describe the itinerary once: dates, headcount, budget ceiling, and what you'll accept without being asked.

02

Msafara runs the outreach

It drafts and sends requests over email or WhatsApp, follows up when a vendor goes quiet, and negotiates within the bounds you set.

03

You get a ranked comparison, then a proposal

Replies land side by side by price as they arrive. Confirmed bookings become a client-ready document automatically.
